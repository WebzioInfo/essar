## 7. SECTION F: VERIFIED CHRONOLOGICAL HISTORY

- **2004:** Founding of Essar Enterprises in South India, focusing on industrial reverse osmosis and water filtration engineering.
- **2004–2015:** Expansion of technical services across Kerala, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu. Practical experience gained running and troubleshooting commercial water plants.
- **2016–2020:** Shift to end-to-end consultancy model ("Plan to Plant") to address common entrepreneur pitfalls (failed BIS audits, improper machinery sizing).
- **2021:** Execution of KENBY bare-land turnkey setup for Eranad Beverages Pvt Ltd.
- **2022:** Setup of INSTAPANI bottling line and takeover of operational management for Faiha Beverages in Edavanna, Kerala.
- **2023:** Major revival of closed Gangothri Water facility in Changarakulam, Kerala, restoring commercial 20L jar distribution under Essar management.
- **2024–2026:** Launch of active project setups in Tirur (Greenmount) and Ponnani (Greenway), bringing overall supported facilities to 50+.
