- **Subtitle:** Architectural Layouts Built for BIS Approval
- **Description:** Expert civil engineering layouts focused on hygienic workflow, epoxy flooring, clean rooms, and drainage systems for regulatory inspections.
- **Key Deliverables:** 2D Cleanroom Zoning Blueprint, Drainage & Slope Specification, Air Shower & Changing Room Layout, BIS Audit Readiness.

### 3. In-House Laboratory Setup (`/services/laboratory`)
- **Subtitle:** Chemical & Microbiological Testing Facilities
- **Description:** Procurement, installation, and calibration of chemical and microbiological lab equipment for daily quality control.
- **Key Deliverables:** Chemical & Microbiological Instrument Procurement, Lab Desk & Reagent Setup, Chemist Training Manuals, BIS Audit Reagent Verification.

- **Subtitle:** Regulatory Planning and Audit Support
- **Description:** Application drafting, document compilation, pre-audit readiness reviews, and factory inspection coordination for ISI (IS 14543) and FSSAI.
- **Key Deliverables:** BIS ISI Application Filing, FSSAI Manufacturing License Drafting, Groundwater NOC Support, Pre-Audit Mock Inspection.

### 5. Brand Registration & Label Design (`/services/branding`)
- **Subtitle:** Create a Premium Water Brand
- **Description:** Trademark coordination, bottle shape selection, vendor decisions, and label guidance aligned to packaged drinking water compliance needs.
- **Key Deliverables:** Trademark Application Support, Custom PET Bottle Mold CAD Alignment, BIS-Compliant Label Artwork, Vendor Procurement Matrix.
