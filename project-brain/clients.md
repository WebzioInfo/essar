# Customer Personas
1. **Investor:** Goals: ROI, passive income. Pain Points: Licensing risk. Objections: High initial capital.
2. **Entrepreneur:** Goals: Fast time-to-market. Pain Points: Technical complexity. 
3. **Plant Owner:** Goals: Upgrade capacity, reduce breakdown. Pain Points: High maintenance.
4. **Water Brand Owner:** Goals: Premium quality, shelf-appeal. Pain Points: Consistency.
5. **Commercial RO Operator:** Goals: High throughput, low TDS.
