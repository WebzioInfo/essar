# Essar Services
Essar Enterprises provides A-Z Turnkey Factory Setup Services for the water industry.

## Core Services
1. **Turnkey Plant Setup:** Complete execution from bare land to first commercial bottle. Includes machinery sizing, installation, and commissioning.
2. **Plant Design & Civil Work Layout:** Expert guidance on hygienic factory design, epoxy flooring, drainage, and clean-room requirements to meet BIS standards.
3. **Laboratory Setup:** Installation of in-house chemical and microbiological testing labs as mandated by ISI/BIS.
4. **QC Training:** Training for plant chemists and operators on water testing and machine maintenance.
5. **Licensing Support:** Complete handling of the complex BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) and FSSAI (Food Safety) documentation and approval process.
6. **Branding Support:** Custom bottle design, label printing, and brand registration assistance.
7. **Plant Modernization:** Upgrading existing RO plants to higher capacities (e.g., 2000 LPH to 6000 LPH) with modern automation.
